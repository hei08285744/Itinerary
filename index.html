Your account

Shared trips

Your content

You keep ownership of content you add. You allow Mytinerary to store, process, and display it only as needed to provide trip planning and collaboration.

Travel information

Availability

The service is provided as available and may change or experience interruptions. To the extent allowed by law, Mytinerary is not responsible for losses caused by relying on unverified trip information.

Ending use

You may stop using Mytinerary at any time. Access may be limited for serious misuse or security reasons.

MYTINERARY

Privacy Notice

Effective September 4, 2026

What we collect

Mytinerary uses only your Firebase user ID to recognize your account. It also stores the traveler personality, profile name, trips, itineraries, expenses, and collaboration details you choose to add.

What we do not collect

Mytinerary does not copy your Google profile photo into your app profile and never receives or stores your Google password. It does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, or other private Google services.

How information is used

Information is used to sign you in, keep trips available across devices, synchronize shared trips, and operate app features.

Who can see trip data

People who are authorized for a shared trip can see its shared content. Firebase, Google Maps, and other service providers process limited data needed to deliver their features.

Storage and security

Authentication is handled by Google and Firebase over HTTPS. Firebase session tokens may be stored on your device to keep you signed in. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may stop signing in, remove content you control, or request account-data deletion from the app operator. Removing access may prevent shared trips from working across devices.
